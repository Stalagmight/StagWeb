Core Capabilities

Crystalline Precision

Every facet of your underwater survey workflow. Click to unfold each capability.

Direct import from mNemo dive computers. Parse DMP survey files with automatic station detection, compass bearings, and depth readings. Batch import supported for processing multiple dives at once.

Manual data entry via a built-in spreadsheet interface. Enter station names, azimuths, distances, and depths with validation, auto-complete, and undo history. Designed for speed in the field.

Render surveys as interactive 2D plan and profile views. Overlay on satellite imagery with real-world coordinates. Pan, zoom, and measure with precision tools.

Organize complex cave systems into logical sections. Color-code passages, manage branching networks, and merge independent survey trips seamlessly.

Automatic detection of loop closures in survey networks. Identify redundant paths, calculate misclosure vectors, and validate network consistency.

Distribute accumulated survey errors across loop closures using least-squares adjustment. Improve positional accuracy across your entire cave network.

Workflow

From Raw Data to Map

A crystalline pipeline in five steps. Expand each to explore the process.

Connect your mNemo device and import DMP files directly, or enter survey legs manually via the built-in spreadsheet. All data validated on entry with instant error feedback.

Group survey legs into logical cave sections. Assign colors, names, and metadata. Build the structural skeleton of your cave map with drag-and-drop organization.

The engine automatically finds loops in your survey network. Review misclosure errors and flag problem legs for re-survey or correction. Visual highlighting of detected loops.

Apply least-squares error distribution across all detected loops. Watch your network tighten as positional accuracy improves across every station in real time.

View your survey in 2D with satellite overlay. Pan, zoom, and annotate. Export finished maps as PNG, SVG, PDF, or data as CSV and JSON for publication or further analysis.

Feature Highlights

Every Facet Refined

Built by cave divers, for cave divers. Every detail polished to crystalline clarity.

Satellite Overlay

Georeference your surveys and overlay them on satellite imagery. See exactly where passages run relative to surface features.

Tauri + Rust Core

Native performance with a lightweight footprint. The Rust backend handles computation while React delivers a fluid interface.

Multi-Format Export

Export maps as high-resolution PNG, scalable SVG, or print-ready PDF. Export raw data as CSV or structured JSON.

Collaborative Workflow

Merge surveys from multiple dive teams. Import and combine independent survey trips into a unified cave network.

Statistical Analysis

View detailed statistics for each survey section: total length, depth range, average leg length, and misclosure percentages.

Offline-First Design

No internet required. Process surveys in the field, at base camp, or on the boat. Your data stays local and secure.
