Survey Station GIS

Manage survey stations with full GIS data integration. Import coordinates, set datums, and maintain spatial accuracy throughout.

Getting Started

From surface to survey
in four steps

Stalagmight guides your team from project creation to published cave maps with a streamlined onboarding workflow.

1

Create Your Project

Set up a new cave system project, define the coordinate reference system, and establish your survey datum. Import existing data or start fresh.

2

Assemble Your Team

Invite team members and assign roles. Designate survey leads, data editors, and equipment managers with granular permission controls.

3

Map and Survey

Deploy to the field with mobile-ready tools. Record survey legs, capture passage dimensions, photograph formations, and log equipment placements in real time.

Analyze and Share
