Core Capabilities

Everything you need to
map the underground

StalagMight turns raw survey data into navigable, shareable cave maps. Built for the demands of underwater cave exploration.

DMP Import

Import survey data directly from mNemo dive computers. Automatic parsing of DMP file format with validation and error reporting.

Spreadsheet Entry

Manual data entry with a familiar spreadsheet interface. Input compass bearings, distances, depths, and station notes with ease.

2D Visualization

Interactive plan-view map with satellite imagery overlay. Pan, zoom, rotate and measure your survey network in real time.

Section Management

Organize surveys into logical sections. Group, color-code, and toggle visibility of cave passages for cleaner cartography.

Loop Closure

Automatic detection of closed loops in your survey network. Identify discrepancies and validate data integrity instantly.

Multi-Format Export

Export your maps and data to PNG, SVG, PDF for print-ready cartography, or CSV/JSON for analysis and integration.

Getting Started

From raw data to finished map

Five steps from dive reel to publication-ready cartography.

1

Import Your Survey

Load DMP files from your mNemo device, or open the spreadsheet editor to enter bearings, distances, and depths manually.

2

Organize Sections

Group survey legs into named sections. Assign colors, add notes, and structure your cave system logically.

3

Visualize & Overlay

See your survey network rendered on an interactive 2D map. Toggle satellite imagery overlay for geographic context.

4

Close Loops & Distribute Errors

StalagMight detects closed loops automatically. Review discrepancies and distribute measurement errors across the network.

5

Export & Share

Generate print-ready maps in PNG, SVG, or PDF. Export raw data as CSV or JSON for further analysis or collaboration.
