Survey Instruments

Chart the Depths

DMP File Import

Import survey data directly from mNemo diving devices. Automatic parsing of bearing, distance, and depth readings from your underwater expeditions.

Spreadsheet Entry

Manual data entry through an intuitive spreadsheet interface. Enter station names, compass bearings, distances, and depth data with full validation.

2D Visualization

Render your cave surveys as detailed 2D plan views with satellite imagery overlay. Pan, zoom, and examine every passage and connection.

Section Management

Organize complex cave systems into logical sections. Group survey legs, assign names, and manage hierarchical relationships between passages.

Loop Closure Detection

Automatically identify closed loops in your survey network. Essential for detecting errors and validating the integrity of your cave survey data.

Error Distribution

Distribute survey measurement errors across loop closures using least-squares adjustment. Achieve the most accurate representation of cave geometry.

Field Protocol

Your Expedition Route

I

Gather Your Data

Import DMP files from your mNemo device after a dive, or manually enter survey measurements through the built-in spreadsheet. All standard cave survey data fields are supported.

II

Organize Sections

Structure your cave system by creating sections for each passage and branch. Name your stations, annotate survey legs, and build a clear hierarchy of your underwater cave network.

III

Detect & Correct

Let StalagMight identify loop closures in your survey. The error distribution engine uses least-squares adjustment to optimally spread measurement discrepancies across the network.

IV

Visualize & Map

Explore your cave survey in the 2D viewer with satellite overlay. Examine passage connectivity, verify loop integrity, and review the complete map of your underwater exploration.

V

Export & Share

Generate publication-ready outputs in PNG, SVG, or PDF for printed maps, or export structured data in CSV and JSON for further analysis and collaboration with fellow explorers.
