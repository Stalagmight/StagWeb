The Route

Your Expedition Begins

From basecamp to the deepest sump, Stalagmight guides your team every step of the way.

I

Establish Basecamp

Create your project, invite your team, and configure roles. Set your datum, coordinate system, and cave entrance coordinates.

Est. setup time: 10 minutes
II

Import Survey Data

Upload existing surveys in standard formats, import GPS tracks, or begin fresh with our integrated survey instrument interface.

Supports Survex, Therion, Compass, CSV
III

Map the Passages

Build your cave map collaboratively. Add survey stations, draw passages, annotate formations, and link scientific observations.

Real-time collaboration enabled
IV

Analyze & Share

Generate reports, export publication-ready maps, share findings with the speleological community, and plan your next expedition.

Export: PDF, SVG, GeoJSON, KML
Cartographic Engine

Survey-Grade Mapping

Our mapping engine renders cave systems from raw survey data with loop closure, error distribution, and automatic passage wall generation.

  • Least-squares loop closure algorithm
  • LRUD passage wall generation
  • Vertical section and extended elevation views
  • Layer management for geological features
Spatial Intelligence

GIS-Integrated Station Network

Every survey station carries full GIS metadata. Transform between coordinate systems, overlay geological maps, and perform spatial queries.

  • Support for 4,000+ coordinate reference systems
  • Geological map overlay and cross-referencing
  • Surface-to-cave spatial correlation
  • Hydrological flow path analysis
