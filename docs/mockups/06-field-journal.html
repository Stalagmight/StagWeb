Field Notes

detailed observations
Import

DMP File Import

Connect your mNemo dive computer and import DMP files directly. Station names, distances, bearings, and depth readings are parsed automatically. No manual transcription errors.

Data Entry

Spreadsheet Interface

Full spreadsheet editor for manual survey entry. Inline validation catches errors as you type. Edit any measurement, add notes, flag questionable readings.

Visualization

2D Map + Satellite

Interactive canvas renders your survey in 2D with satellite imagery overlay. Pan, zoom, measure. Real-time updates as you edit data. Color-coded sections for clarity.

Organization

Section Management

Group survey legs into logical named sections. Color-code passages, merge branches, split complex surveys. Annotate with field notes directly on the map.

Accuracy

Loop Closure

Automatic detection of closed traverses in your survey network. Configurable error distribution spreads accumulated errors for maximum geometric accuracy.

Export

Multi-Format Output

Publication-ready maps in PNG, SVG, PDF. Raw data export in CSV, JSON for GIS integration, research databases, or collaboration with other survey teams.

Why This Matters

important

Native Performance

Built with Tauri and Rust for fast, native execution. React-powered UI stays responsive even with thousands of survey stations. No Electron bloat.

Precision Accuracy

Advanced loop closure algorithms detect and distribute accumulated errors. Configurable weighting ensures your survey maps reflect the actual cave geometry.

Offline-First

No internet required. Process data in the field, at camp, or in the lab. Your survey data stays on your machine, always accessible and always private.

key feature

Open Formats

Import from mNemo DMP files. Export to industry-standard PNG, SVG, PDF, CSV, JSON. Integrate with GIS tools, research databases, or other survey software.
