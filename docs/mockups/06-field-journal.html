Methodology — Getting Started

The Survey Method

Four entries in your logbook and you're mapping underground. Here's the procedure.

1

Establish Base Camp

Create your cave system project. Define the entrance coordinates, set your coordinate reference system, and establish the datum. This is your survey origin — everything references back here.

← Takes about 2 minutes!
2

Assemble Your Team

Invite team members by email. Assign roles: survey lead, data entry, equipment manager, or administrator. Everyone gets the access they need and nothing more.

Supports unlimited collaborators ^
3

Import Field Data

Upload existing survey files (Therion, Compass, Walls), GPS tracks, and field notes. Or start fresh with our survey entry forms that mirror your paper logbook.

$ stalagmight import --format therion survey_data.th
4

Analyze & Publish

Generate publication-ready maps, run spatial analyses, and share your findings. Export to SVG, PDF, or GIS formats. Your logbook becomes a research paper.

See "Results" section below ↓
