Getting Started

From Dive Data
to Published Map

1

Install the Application

Download StalagMight for Windows, macOS, or Linux. Built on Tauri with a Rust backend, it installs in seconds with a minimal footprint.

2

Import Your Survey Data

Load DMP files directly from your mNemo device or enter data manually through the spreadsheet interface. Both paths feed the same analysis engine.

3

Organize into Sections

Structure your cave system into sections and sub-sections. Tag stations, name passages, and build a logical hierarchy of your survey network.

4

Analyze and Refine

The system detects loop closures and distributes accumulated errors automatically. Review, adjust, and ensure sub-meter accuracy throughout.

5

Visualize and Export

View your survey as an interactive 2D map with satellite overlay. Export publication-ready outputs in PNG, SVG, PDF, or raw data as CSV/JSON.
