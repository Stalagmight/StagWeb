Core Capabilities

Tools Forged in the Deep

Every feature built for the unique demands of underground survey work, from cave floor to data center.

3D Cave Mapping

Full three-dimensional passage modeling with cross-sections, geological annotations, and multi-level topology support.

GIS Survey Stations

Precise geospatial station management with multi-datum transforms, UTM grids, and coordinate reference system support.

Equipment Tracking

Monitor sensors, cylinders, rigging gear, and instruments across expeditions with maintenance logs and deployment histories.

Experiment Management

Track scientific experiments linked to survey stations, cave features, and time-series data collection campaigns.

GPS Track Import

Import GPX and KML surface tracks, align with underground surveys, and build unified topographic context maps.

Team Collaboration

Role-based permissions, real-time session sharing, data merge with conflict resolution, and expedition crew management.
