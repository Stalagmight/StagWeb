Core Capabilities

Everything you need underground

From raw survey data to publication-ready maps. StalagMight handles the complete underwater cave survey workflow.

DMP File Import

Import survey data directly from mNemo dive computers. Automatic parsing of station names, distances, bearings, and depth readings.

Spreadsheet Entry

Manually enter and edit survey data through an intuitive spreadsheet interface. Full control over every measurement with inline validation.

2D Visualization

Interactive 2D map rendering with satellite imagery overlay. Pan, zoom, and explore your survey data with real-time updates.

Section Management

Organize survey lines into logical sections. Color-code, merge, split, and annotate survey segments for clarity.

Loop Closure & Error Distribution

Automatic loop closure detection with configurable error distribution algorithms. Minimize accumulated survey errors across complex networks.

Multi-Format Export

Export finished maps and data to PNG, SVG, PDF for publication or CSV, JSON for further analysis and integration.

Workflow

From dive to map in four steps

StalagMight streamlines the entire cave survey pipeline into a clear, intuitive process.

Step 01

Import Your Survey Data

Plug in your mNemo device and import DMP files directly, or enter survey measurements manually using the built-in spreadsheet. StalagMight parses station names, distances, compass bearings, and depth profiles automatically.

Step 02

Visualize & Overlay

See your survey rendered as a 2D line plot in real time. Toggle satellite imagery underneath to verify alignment with known geography. Pan, zoom, rotate, and measure directly on the canvas.

Step 03

Organize & Correct

Group survey legs into named sections. Run loop closure detection to find closed traverses, then distribute accumulated errors across the network for maximum accuracy.
