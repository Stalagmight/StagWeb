Capabilities

Everything You Need Underground

A complete toolkit for speleological teams, from initial survey to published research. Every feature designed for the cave environment.

Cave System Mapping

Interactive 2D and 3D mapping of cave passages, chambers, and geological features with precision survey data integration.

Survey Station Management

Complete GIS-enabled station management with coordinate systems, datums, and precise positional data for every measurement point.

Equipment Tracking

Monitor sensors, gas cylinders, climbing gear, and instruments. Track calibration schedules, battery levels, and deployment locations.

Scientific Experiments

Design, track, and manage research studies with structured data collection, environmental monitoring, and result analysis tools.

GPS Track Import

Import GPX files and GPS tracks from surface surveys. Overlay surface topography with underground passages for complete spatial context.

Team Collaboration

Role-based permissions, real-time data sync, team dashboards, and coordinated field sessions. Built for multi-party expeditions.

Onboarding

Surface to Survey in Minutes

Getting started is as natural as entering a cave. Four simple steps to illuminate your data.

1

Create Your Cave

Name your cave system, set the coordinate reference, and define the entrance points. Your digital cave begins here.

2

Build Your Team

Invite surveyors, scientists, and collaborators. Assign roles for data entry, review, and administration.

3

Import & Survey

Analyze & Share
