Core capabilities

Features

Everything you need to go from raw survey data to a finished map.

F-01

Free Forever

No trials, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Stalagmight is and always will be completely free to use.

F-02

Open-Source

All source code is publicly available. Inspect it, modify it, or contribute back to the project.

F-03

mNemo & Manual Data Entry

Import DMP files directly from mNemo survey device, or enter data by hand with a built-in spreadsheet.

F-04

Automated Section Alignment

Automatically match and connect survey sections based on shared reference shots. Less manual placement, fewer mistakes, faster data entry.

F-05

2D Visualization

Interactive plan view with satellite overlay. See your survey on a real map.

F-06

Loop Closure

Detect loops in your survey network and distribute errors automatically for more accurate maps.

Import workflow

From DMP File to Mapped Cave

A guided wizard walks you through every step of importing mNemo survey data — from file selection to final placement on your cave map.

01

Section Selection

p. 12

Open a DMP file and review its contents at a glance — shot count, total length, depth range. Pick which sections to import, skip already-imported ones, and set default surveyor and date for the batch.

02

Zero Datum Configuration

p. 13

Anchor your first section to real-world coordinates. Choose a station as the GPS zero datum, enter coordinates in decimal degrees, and configure section metadata — name, surveyor, date, color, and direction.

03

Connection Suggesting

p. 14

For each subsequent section, the system automatically suggests a connection point based on shot matching. Review the confidence level, merge-station mapping, and overlap handling — or pick from alternative connections.

04

Manual Placing

p. 15

When no automatic suggestion fits, take full control. Choose from previous suggestions, connect to an existing station, place manually on the map, or skip the section entirely. Full shot table and metadata remain editable.
