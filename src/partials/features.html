Features

Everything you need to go from raw survey data to a finished map.

Free and Open-Source

No trials, no subscriptions, no paywalls — Stalagmight is free to use and always will be. All source code is public, so you can inspect it, modify it, or contribute back.

mNemo & Manual Data Entry

Import DMP files directly from mNemo survey device, or enter data by hand with a built-in spreadsheet.

Visual-First Editing

Connect surveyed sections together visually, enter comments, add loop closures and edit survey data with very little need for tabular data entry. StalagMight encourages intuitive survey building and assists with section placement.

SpeleoDB Support

Collaborate with other StalagMight users on SpeleoDB and enjoy its versioning and backup features.

Dive Planning

Use the survey data to plan future dives, estimate stage drop points and penetration range.

Import & Export Existing Data

Read existing survey files instead of retyping them — Ariane, Compass, Walls and GPX. Export finished maps as SVG, PNG or PDF, and the survey data itself as CSV, JSON or Ariane TML.
