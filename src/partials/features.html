Core Capabilities

Direct import from mNemo dive computers. Parse DMP survey files with automatic station detection, compass bearings, and depth readings.

Manual data entry via a built-in spreadsheet interface. Enter station names, azimuths, distances, and depths with validation.

Organize complex cave systems into logical sections. Color-code passages, manage branching networks, and merge survey trips.

Automatic detection of loop closures in survey networks. Identify redundant paths, calculate misclosure vectors.

Error Distribution

Distribute accumulated survey errors across loop closures using least-squares adjustment. Improve positional accuracy.
