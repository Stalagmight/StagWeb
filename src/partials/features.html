Core Capabilities

Features

Everything you need to go from raw survey data to a finished map.

Free Forever

No trials, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Stalagmight is and always will be completely free to use.

Open-Source

All source code is publicly available. Inspect it, modify it, or contribute back to the project.

mNemo & Manual Data Entry

Import DMP files directly from mNemo survey device, or enter data by hand with a built-in spreadsheet.

Visual-First Editing

Build the map by working on it directly. Drag a station, a single shot, or a whole branch, with live preview and snapping as you go — and the table below is the same data, so nothing gets out of step.

Dive Planning

Answer the question you actually care about: on this gas, starting here, how far can you get before you have to turn? Stations shade by the gas they cost to reach, with turn points and stage drops marked along the route.

Works With Your Existing Tools

Open surveys from Ariane, Compass, Walls and GPX instead of starting over. Send finished maps out as SVG, PNG or PDF, and your data as TML, CSV or JSON.
